Insights

The chart shows how reported COVID-19 cases and vaccinations changed over time for each country. Case counts rise and fall in waves, while vaccination values generally increase later in the timeline. Some sharp peaks may reflect real outbreaks, but they may also come from delayed or batch reporting.

These trends should be interpreted carefully. Changes in vaccinations and cases do not prove a direct cause-and-effect relationship, since case counts are also influenced by testing levels, reporting practices, public health policy, and behavior.

A line chart was used because it clearly shows change over time. Axis labels, title, legend, and caption were kept simple for readability. Missing vaccination values were interpolated to preserve continuity, and mild outliers were clipped rather than removed so unusual spikes remain visible without dominating the figure.
